Anas Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Anas Publisher is a private tool used only by Anas Ali to publish his own content to his own Instagram account (@withanasali) and his own Facebook Page ("Anas Ali - أنس علي"). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app uses

The app uses access tokens that Anas Ali grants to it, and the content he chooses to publish (videos, images and captions). It also reads basic information about his own accounts and posts, such as post IDs, links and engagement counts, to confirm that a post went live and to show him his results.

What the app does not do

The app does not collect, sell or share personal data about other people. It does not read private messages for any purpose other than the owner's own account management, and it does not use data for advertising.

Storage

Tokens and publishing records are stored privately for the owner's use only and are removed when no longer needed.

Data deletion

To remove the app's access, remove "Anas Publisher" from Facebook Settings → Business Integrations. To request deletion of any stored data, email the owner at the contact listed on the Facebook Page; all related data will be deleted within 30 days.

Contact
